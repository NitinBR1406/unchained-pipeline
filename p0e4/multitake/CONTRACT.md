# MULTI_TAKE_RAW_DROP_V01

This additive contract accepts multiple byte-bound performance takes, exactly one authoritative master audio asset, and optional photos. It is restricted to `SYNTHETIC_DISPOSABLE_ONLY` until Nitin authorizes a real drop.

Camera/guide audio may establish offset, drift, confidence, and usable-range evidence. It is always muted in the final programme; the bound master is the only programme audio. Every take must pass the declared confidence, drift, anchor, decode, and usable-range criteria. A failing take holds the synchronized set rather than being silently discarded.

The EDL is contiguous, time-bounded, SHA-bound, filename-independent, and enforces the 80% Performance Rule and four-derivative maximum. Technical normalization and shot matching remain separate from the creative UNCHAINED look. Captions and titles require an authoritative text hash. Output QC covers sync, cuts, crop/framing, effect density, color match, audio, caption safe area, decode, and performer visibility. Every repair has a two-attempt ceiling and requires independent re-QC.

Validated Gemini, Claude, and Resolve artifacts in synthetic evidence are contract fixtures only. They do not claim external-agent execution, real rendering, creative approval, rights clearance, deployment, or publication authority.
