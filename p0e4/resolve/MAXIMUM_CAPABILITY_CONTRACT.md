# Master Finishing Engine — additive V02

ResolveAdapter compiles canonical data and exact operation details into a master
finishing plan. The V01 renderer profile remains a component, not the scope of
the adapter. No accepted V16.1 evidence or canonical schema is rewritten.

Full mapping:
- cuts/timing: exact source and timeline frame bounds in base.jobs[].cuts;
- reframes, zooms/push-ins, shakes, captions, transitions and effects: existing
  strict operation supplement, with referenced bytes now verified;
- lyric/text emphasis: exact cue text, half-open frame intervals and Unicode
  code-point spans, plus hash-bound style and source evidence;
- color/look: hash-bound locked baseline preservation; changes require separate
  Nitin change control, even though the engine design covers grading;
- audio sync: source SHA, integer sample rate and sample offset, preserved gain,
  pitch and rate. Offsets are recommendations pending source binding and lip-sync
  QC, not authority to alter the locked final audio;
- platform crops/render profiles: existing validated aspect/dimensions/fps;
- QC/rework: exact output-byte checks and existing independent-QC receipt loop.

API_SCRIPTABLE, EXTERNAL_INTELLIGENCE, FUSION_FAIRLIGHT and GUI_ONLY are design
route categories. Effective routes remain UNSUPPORTED_FAIL_CLOSED until actual
installed version, method signatures and bounded safe smokes establish reliability.
Fusion Python/Lua is documented. Fairlight timeline automation does not prove an
external Fairlight API. No API names have been invented. Computer Use is only a
future bounded fallback after API absence and UI reliability are demonstrated.
No supplied booleans, product marketing or typed hash alone enable a transport.

The compiler preserves all 14 canonical groups and their source hashes. Experiment,
hypothesis, campaign and business attribution remain in the canonical Factory;
Resolve does not become a second analytics, approval or publication datastore.
The 10M plan requires measurement, versioned experiments and reusable finishing,
not an assumed codec, license or revenue outcome. Daily throughput remains to be
measured on installed hardware. Free/Studio matrix V02 distinguishes product
features from integration reliability and missing evidence; purchase stays Nitin's.
