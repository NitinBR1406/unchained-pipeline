Sample Selection Rule

Timeframe: Released between January 2011 and September 2026.

Commercial Milestone: ≥ 300 Million views on official YouTube video uploads and/or top-tier Spotify India streaming figures.

Traceability: Verified against official audio/music video metadata, DSP registrations, and verified harmonic transcribers (Tunebat, Songstats, Chordify verified sheets, acoustic pitch detection).

Deduplication: Film audio duplicates, slowed/reverb remixes, lofi versions, and unauthorized uploads were purged; only original master releases are analyzed.
