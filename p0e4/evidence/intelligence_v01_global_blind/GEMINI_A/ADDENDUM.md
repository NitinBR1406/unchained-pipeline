3. Revised Vocal Range and Tessitura Qualification (Nitin)
The vocal evaluation protocol is updated to prevent vocal fatigue and ensure acoustically valid vocal profiling:
Elimination of Maximum-Pitch Stress Tests: Nitin must not be directed to sing into phonatory instability or acoustic break points (passaggio fractures). Forcing phonation at the extreme upper boundary induces laryngeal tension, false vocal fold constriction, and registration strain.
Distinction Between Climax Pitch and Tessitura: Sustaining or touching an isolated peak note (e.g., in a brief melismatic flourish or climax) does not demonstrate tessitura fit. Tessitura constitutes the comfortable pitch zone where the singer maintains:
Consistent timbre, acoustic resonance, and vowel clarity without formant tracking failure.
Sustainable subglottic pressure without hyperfunction over extended lyrical phrasing.
Dynamic elasticity (ability to execute both mezza voce and forte).
Correct Protocol: Tessitura evaluation must be conducted through mid-register scalar exercises, phrase repetition at varied dynamic levels, and assessment of vocal comfort over repeated cycle iterations rather than peak-extension trials.
